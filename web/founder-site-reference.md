### The crisis-origin narrative (4-stage table)
Intro: "Before the crisis, there were moments. Quiet ones. Easy to miss. Easier to ignore. Every crisis has an origin story. DCI exists in the gap between the first sign and the breaking point: the space where the outcome can still change."

Columns: **Stage / What it looks like / What happens when nobody catches it / What DCI does here**

**1. Pressure builds** — "Work. School. Family. Money. All of it at once."
- Looks like: "Stress that looks like ambition. Worry that looks like being responsible. Isolation that looks like being busy."
- If nobody catches it: "The guy who was 'always hustling' doesn't realise he hasn't had a real conversation in three months. Nobody asks because he looks like he's winning."
- DCI (**CONNECT**): "A Community Champion creates the kind of space where someone can say 'actually, I'm not okay,' before they've stopped believing it themselves."

**2. Warning signals** — "The body starts keeping score before the mind does."
- Looks like: "Not sleeping. Snapping at people for no reason. Drinking a little more than usual. Scrolling until 2am because being alone with your thoughts feels worse."
- If nobody catches it: "Part after part is all fun, until it isn't. Until the drink is the only thing that makes the room feel manageable. Until you can't raw dog a Tuesday anymore without something to take the edge off. That's not a personality. That's a signal nobody caught."
- DCI (**EQUIP**): "The Mood Tracker reads what people can't yet say. The Card Game opens the room. The Journal gives language to something that's been sitting nameless for months."

**3. The support gap** — "The hardest, most expensive place to be stuck."
- Looks like: "'I know something's wrong.' 'I don't know who to tell.' 'I don't want to be dramatic.' 'I'll figure it out.' Three months pass."
- If nobody catches it: "The family that spent UGX 12 million on rehab didn't see it coming. Or they did, and didn't know what to do with what they saw. By the time fentanyl, prescription pills, or alcohol has its grip, you're not treating a bad habit. You're fighting for someone's life. And rehab is just the beginning of a bill that never fully stops."
- DCI (**UNDERSTAND**): "The Early Warning System reads patterns across the whole community, so support doesn't wait for someone to ask. It's already moving toward them."

**4. Crisis** — "When the cost stops being abstract."
- Looks like: "Burnout that empties a person. Addiction that restructures a family. A funeral that nobody saw coming, and everyone will spend years turning over in their mind."
- If nobody catches it: "'Why didn't I see it coming?' That question doesn't go away. The parent who replays every dinner. The friend who scrolls back through WhatsApp looking for the message they missed. The manager who gave the last performance review. Losing someone to suicide doesn't end with them, it lives in everyone who loved them, forever."
- DCI (**RESPOND**): "DCI doesn't replace clinical care or crisis services. It means fewer people arrive here. And for those who do, the pathway to appropriate support is already mapped."

Closing line: "DCI doesn't compete with mental health organisations. It's what communities need before they need them. Every toolkit used, every Champion trained, every conversation held earns a Connection Mile™: proof that support reached a real person, before the crisis did."
